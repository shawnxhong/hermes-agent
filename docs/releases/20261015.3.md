## Known issue: FEISHU-MEDIA-STOP-LATENCY

User reports that Feishu `stop playback` does stop playback, but sometimes
takes more than 20 seconds. Accepted as an open performance issue, not fixed.
Frequency and root cause have not been measured. Do not attribute the delay
to the LLM, Feishu transport, scheduling or player shutdown without evidence.
Future investigation should correlate inbound message, inference/tool dispatch,
media RPC and player-exit timestamps. This release adds no workaround.
